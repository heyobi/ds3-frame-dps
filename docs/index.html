What the number means. Damage per second while chaining the light attack, no buffs. Against a flat defense every damage type uses the same defense and no absorption. Pick a boss to use its real HP, defense and absorption per damage type (NG, read from the game's regulation), and to add bleed and frostbite procs. Poison, hyper armor and reach are not counted. "1H R1" is one-handed R1, "2H R1" two-handed R1, "L1 dual" the two-handed L1 of paired weapons and fists.

The stat search here is finer than the 3-point grid used for the repo tables, so a weapon can come out 1 to 5 DPS higher than in the README. Motion values, hit counts and stamina costs come from the game's own attack params; weapon damage and scaling from DS3InventoryBuilder. Bosses take a reduced share of bleed damage (usually 50%); the same share is applied to frostbite, which is not confirmed in game. Status buildup is assumed not to decay between hits.

Click a weapon to see a leveling path with a DPS chart. The link in the address bar keeps your inputs, so you can share a build.
