Dark Souls III DPS calculator

Sustained light-attack DPS for every weapon and infusion, using attack and cancel frames read from the game files (App 1.15.2, Regulation 1.35, both DLCs). Enter your stats, or a soul level to get the best stat spread per weapon. Method and raw data: github.com/heyobi/ds3-frame-dps
New: Elden Ring version

Stats
The points left after Vig/Att/End/Vit go to Str/Dex/Int/Fth/Lck on top of the class's starting stats, placed separately for each weapon.
Include ring bonuses in the numbers. Two-handing counts Str × 1.5.
Target
Boss and unique weapons go to +5; they use half the level shown (+10 → +5, +7 → +3).
Stamina limited: long fights where you keep attacking. Stamina does not refill while attacking and refills at 45/s otherwise, so each point spent costs 1/45 s.
Buffs and rings
Spell buff comes from the catalyst, its upgrade and your Int / Faith, as the equipment screen shows it; a catalyst whose requirements you don't meet adds nothing. "Best I can use" takes the strongest one of the spell's school that your stats allow. Catalysts that stop at +5 use half the upgrade shown, like weapons.
Rings 0/4 (Chloranthy Ring counts too)
Values from the game files. Clutch rings ×1.15 damage of their element, Crown of Dusk ×1.05 magic. Lloyd's, Pontiff's and Flynn's raise attack, Red Tearstone and Clutch rings raise damage. Stat rings count for requirements and are added on top of the spread shown. Buffs only go on weapons with no elemental damage; paired weapons get less from resins and Blessed Weapon (fitted to in-game measurements), spell buffs are not reduced. Great Swamp Ring and Witch's Ring do not raise spell buff or buff damage (tested in game).

#WeaponAttacks / attack ARReachDamage / secondStr/Dex/Int/Fth/LckS/D/I/F/L

What the number means. Damage per second while chaining the light attack, no buffs. Against a flat defense every damage type uses the same defense and no absorption. Pick a boss to use its real HP, defense and absorption per damage type (read from the game's regulation, NG or any NG+ cycle), and to add bleed and frostbite procs. Hit zone: some bosses take extra damage on a weak point or body part (Midir x2.0, King of the Storm x1.3); the rates come from the boss's NpcParam, but which part is which you have to know. Poison and hyper armor are not counted. Reach = hand to the far end of the light attack's hitbox (weapon model + attack params), and with the step: plus how far the character has moved forward (root motion of the attack animation) when the hit starts. The arm reaching out is in neither. Reach does not change the DPS. "1H R1" is one-handed R1, "2H R1" two-handed R1, "L1 dual" the two-handed L1 of paired weapons and fists.

The stat search here is finer than the 3-point grid used for the repo tables, so a weapon can come out 1 to 5 DPS higher than in the README. Motion values, hit counts and stamina costs come from the game's own attack params; weapon damage and scaling from DS3InventoryBuilder. Bosses take a reduced share of bleed damage (usually 50%); the same share is applied to frostbite, which is not confirmed in game. Status buildup is assumed not to decay between hits.
