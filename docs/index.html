#WeaponAttacks / attack ARDPSStr/Dex/Int/Fth/LckS/D/I/F/L

What the number means. Damage per second while chaining the light attack, against a flat defense applied to every damage type, no buffs. Bleed, poison and frost are not counted, nor stamina, hyper armor or reach. "1H R1" is one-handed R1, "2H R1" two-handed R1, "L1 dual" the two-handed L1 of paired weapons and fists.

The stat search here is finer than the 3-point grid used for the repo tables, so a weapon can come out 1 to 5 DPS higher than in the README. Motion values come from the community AtkParam sheet; weapon damage and scaling from DS3InventoryBuilder.

The link in the address bar keeps your inputs, so you can share a build.
